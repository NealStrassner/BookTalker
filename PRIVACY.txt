BookTalker privacy policy

BookTalker collects nothing about you. It has no account, no sign-in, no tracking, no analytics
and no advertising, and it sends nothing about you or your books anywhere.

Everything BookTalker does with your books happens on your own computer: reading aloud, text
recognition and translation. Your books, bookmarks, notes and settings stay in your own user
folder.

BookTalker goes online only for these:

- Downloads you choose. Extra voices and the optional AI translator come straight from their
  public sources (huggingface.co, github.com, files.pythonhosted.org) when you pick them. Only
  the files themselves are requested.
- Update check (the version from GitHub only). When it opens, and when you click About ->
  Check for updates, BookTalker reads the list of releases on its GitHub page. Nothing about
  you is sent. The Microsoft Store version makes no update check: the Store updates it.

Those websites receive what any web request carries, such as your IP address, under their own
privacy policies. BookTalker itself keeps no record of it.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/NealStrassner/BookTalker/issues
